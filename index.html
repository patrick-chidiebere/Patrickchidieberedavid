Biography & Background

The Reality & Journey of Building from Lagos

Self-Taught Developer

Learning software engineering outside traditional academic programs requires a unique kind of discipline. My journey as a programmer started with curiosity and a vision to create tools that make a difference.

Navigating tutorials, documentation, and technical hurdles independently taught me how to break down complex systems, solve problems methodically, and write clean, resilient code.

Resilience in Lagos

Building tech from Lagos, Nigeria comes with real-world challenges—power outages, intermittent connections, and limited initial access to premium software infrastructure.

Rather than viewing these as setbacks, they shaped my work ethic. As I wrote in my Medium publication, "The Reality of Coding in Lagos, Nigeria," building under constraint forces you to plan thoroughly, write efficient code, and value every hour of development.

Writer & Scholar

Beyond writing code, I am a passionate analytical writer. Writing clarifies thinking—a skill directly reflected in my multi-year winning streak at the National Travel Essay Competition (NTEC), KHREF essay excerpts, and the CrowdPen AI challenge.

Winning recognition for persistence reflects my approach to both literature and technology: showing up, refining work constantly, and striving for excellence.
